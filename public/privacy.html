Privacy Policy.

Search is a confidential business. Here is exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and what we will never do with it.

BSP-LEGAL-2026-01BOUNDSEARCH.COMBOB@BOUNDSEARCH.COM
01What we collect

What you give us directly: a name, an email address, a phone number, a company, and whatever context you include when you write to us through the contact form or by email. Client portal access is issued per engagement and records sign-ins and document downloads so engagements stay auditable. The site itself keeps standard server logs — IP address, browser type, pages visited.

02How we use it

To respond when you reach out. To run retained engagements — updates, documents, scheduling. To send advisories and market commentary if you have asked for them; every send includes an unsubscribe. Nothing else.

03What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade contact information. We do not share candidate materials with anyone outside the engagement they were gathered for without consent. Client and candidate identities are confidential by default — in our publications, outcomes are real and verified while identities stay protected.

04If you are a candidate

Your résumé, references, and assessment notes are shared only within the engagement they were gathered for. You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

05Cookies & analytics

The site uses minimal analytics to understand aggregate traffic. No advertising trackers. No cross-site profiles.

06Retention & security

Engagement records are kept as long as professional and legal obligations require, then destroyed. Access is limited to the people doing the work.

07Your choices

Ask what we hold, correct it, or request deletion: bob@boundsearch.com or (267) 265-1792. A person answers — usually the same business day. Material changes to this policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
